Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters

BizTrack is a client-side small-business dashboard. Products, orders, expenses, language preference, and cookie consent are stored in this browser through localStorage.

No account system, analytics tracker, advertising cookie, or server-side personal-data collection is used by this version of the application.

You can remove stored records at any time by clearing this site's browser data. Exported CSV files are created locally and are controlled by the user after download.
